# LinkedIn Organic Content Strategy for B2B SaaS

## Topic Selection

For this project, I chose **LinkedIn organic content strategy for B2B SaaS**.

I selected this topic because LinkedIn is a primary growth channel for B2B companies, especially in SaaS, where personal branding and content can directly drive inbound leads without relying on paid ads or cold outreach.

---

## Selected Creators

I focused on creators who actively use LinkedIn as a growth channel and consistently produce high-performing, strategy-driven content relevant to B2B audiences.

### 1. Justin Welsh

**Why selected:**

* Strong focus on solopreneurs and B2B audience building
* Consistently produces high-engagement posts
* Demonstrates clear positioning around content-driven growth and leverage
* Uses repeatable content patterns (e.g., contrarian hooks, simplicity)

---

### 2. Lara Acosta

**Why selected:**

* Focuses on LinkedIn growth and personal branding in a business context
* Frequently shares results-driven content (e.g., revenue, viral posts)
* Combines authority, education, and audience targeting
* Provides actionable frameworks for content creation

---

### 3. Daniel Murray

**Why selected:**

* Active in B2B marketing and demand generation
* Blends educational content with engaging formats (including humor and memes)
* Demonstrates how personality can be integrated into professional content
* Focuses on making marketing concepts accessible and relatable

---

## Data Collection Approach

After selecting the creators, I collected their recent LinkedIn posts manually.

I then structured the data into an API-like format, including:

* Post metadata (date, URL)
* Content breakdown (hook, summary, key points)
* Content type and tone
* Engagement strategy and pattern tags

This structure allows for deeper analysis of content patterns and can be used to build a repeatable LinkedIn content playbook for B2B SaaS.
